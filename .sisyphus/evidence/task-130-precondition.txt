Crux codegen finding: `prettyplease::unparse` inserts whitespace
INSIDE the macro TokenStream (`onclick : move | _ | count += 1`
instead of the idiomatic `onclick: move |_| count += 1`) but does
NOT delete, reorder, or re-tokenize the body. The dioxus-rsx proc
macro receives an equivalent TokenStream and parses it identically.
